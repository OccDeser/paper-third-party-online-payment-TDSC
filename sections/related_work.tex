\section{Related Work}
%-------------------------------------------------------------------------------

\subsection{Third-Party Payment System}

Prior work on third-party payment security has largely analyzed specific payment scenarios or concrete system implementations.
In in-app payment, \cite{Reynaud2012FreeMarket, Mulliner2014VirtualSwindle} identified flaws in Google's in-app billing service that enable unauthorized free purchases, and \cite{Kumar2020SecurityUPI} uncovered design flaws in the multi-factor authentication of the Unified Payments Interface (UPI) in India.
\cite{Yang2017FlawedImplementations, Yang2019SecurityPayment} proposed security rules for in-app payment services in China and found widespread violations that lead to payment bypass and fraud.
\cite{Mohsen2025PayScan} measured payment libraries in over 10,000 Android apps and found that $71.7\%$ of them rely on outdated or insecure payment SDKs.
In web-based payment, \cite{Wang2011ShopFree} revealed logic vulnerabilities in online shopping platforms that integrate services such as PayPal, and \cite{Sun2014DetectingECommerce, Pellegrino2014TowardBlackBox, Sudhodanan2016AttackPatterns} used symbolic execution and black-box analysis to find payment logic flaws across many web applications.
Other studies applied NLP to payment developer documentation to find logic vulnerabilities~\cite{Chen2019LogicVulnerabilities}, analyzed personal payment systems built on third-party payment infrastructures~\cite{Lou2021PaymentVulnerabilities}, and designed a privacy-preserving mobile payment protocol verified with ProVerif~\cite{Neera2025TrustworthyPayment}.

\subsection{Banking Payment Systems}

Empirical studies revealed security flaws in mobile banking applications~\cite{Paik2010StragglersHerd}, privacy and security issues in deployed systems~\cite{Almeida2012MPaymentsBrazil,Harris2012PrivacySecurityMobile}, and transaction integrity vulnerabilities in branchless banking~\cite{Reaves2015MobileMoneyProblems,Reaves2017MobileMoneyProblems}.
Formal methods have been applied extensively to EMV: De Ruiter et al.~\cite{Ruiter2011FormalEMV} analyzed a variant of EMV with ProVerif, and Basin et al.~\cite{Basin2021EMVStandard} modeled the complete EMV protocol with \Tamarin, explaining real-world attacks such as PIN bypass~\cite{Basin2023AuthenticationFailures} and card brand mixup~\cite{Basin2021CardBrandMixup}.

\subsection{Vulnerabilities in Implementations}

Payment applications on mobile platforms suffer from insecure inter-application communication: improperly exported Android components enable launch hijacking and intent spoofing~\cite{Bhandari2017AndroidInterApp, Sun2023VenomAttackAndroid}, insufficiently validated deep links let attackers hijack payment flows and steal parameters~\cite{Liu2017MeasuringMobileDeep}, and malicious QR codes can trigger sensitive interfaces~\cite{Han2023MedusaAttackQR}.

\subsection{Transaction Authentication}

Regulation and industry standards bind the user's authentication to the details of a transaction.
The regulatory technical standards of PSD2 require dynamic linking: for a remote payment, the authentication code is specific to the amount and the payee, and the payer is made aware of both~\cite{EU2018RTS}.
EMV 3-D Secure lets the card issuer authenticate the cardholder for a card-not-present transaction using transaction data supplied by the merchant~\cite{EMVCo2023ThreeDS}, and Secure Payment Confirmation lets the browser display the payee and the amount and obtain an authenticator assertion over them~\cite{W3C2026SPC}.
Attacks on mobile two-factor authentication and on PSD2 banking apps show that these guarantees weaken when the authentication factor and the transaction run on the same compromised device~\cite{Konoth2016Anywhere, Haupert2019PSD2}.
These mechanisms bind the authorization to the amount and the payee, which an order created through the order oracle reproduces exactly, whereas SCC binds it to the order instance and the account that placed it.

Short authenticated strings let users authenticate a key exchange between two devices by comparing a short value~\cite{Vaudenay2005SAS}, whereas SCC transfers a short code in one direction, from the merchant frontend through the user to the payment system (Section~\ref{sec:countermeasure}).
