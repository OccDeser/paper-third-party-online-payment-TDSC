\section*{Ethical Considerations and Open Science}
All experiments were performed using accounts fully controlled by the authors, without involving real users or accessing real user funds.
The Android measurement study only checked whether payment requests could be intercepted at the communication level; it performed no order tampering and imposed no financial or operational impact on developers or merchants.
Affected applications are reported only in aggregated form, and the protocol models are reconstructed solely from public developer documentation.
The issues were disclosed to Alipay, PSP-B, and affected developers before publication (Section~\ref{sec:real_world}), and we do not release the attack implementation or the list of affected applications.
Our artifact repository~\cite{third_payment_model_2026} contains the formal models of the six protocols and of our countermeasure, the specifications of the security properties, and scripts for reproducing the verification results.
