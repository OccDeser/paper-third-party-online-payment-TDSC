\section*{Ethical Considerations and Open Science}
All experiments used accounts fully controlled by the authors, without involving real users or their funds, and the Android measurement study only checked whether payment requests could be intercepted, without tampering with orders.
Affected applications are reported only in aggregated form, the protocol models are reconstructed from public developer documentation, and the issues were disclosed before publication (Section~\ref{sec:real_world}); we do not release the attack implementation or the list of affected applications.
Our artifact repository~\cite{third_payment_model_2026} contains the formal models of the six protocols and of our countermeasure, the specifications of the security properties, and scripts for reproducing the verification results.
